SKYLINE VECTOR - CHANGES FROM MY DESIGN
=======================================

What I kept from my design
--------------------------
- It is a 2D endless flyer. My jet does NOT fall; it stays at its height until I move it.
- Clicking the top half of the screen moves up one level, and the bottom half moves down one level.
- My gates move up and down, so the player has to predict where the opening will be.
- The score goes up for every gate passed, and the game gets faster over time.
- I have a Start screen, a Game Over screen with the score and high score, and a Play Again button.

What I changed, and why
-----------------------
1. I took out the background music.
   I planned to have background music, but I removed it so my other sounds are
   easy to hear. Instead, I tied the engine hum to the gameplay: its pitch
   rises as the game speeds up, so the sound itself tells the player the game
   is getting harder.

2. I drew my jet as a small red propeller plane.
   I used the free Kenney "Tappy Plane" art pack so that my plane, sky, ground
   and gates all share one art style. It still behaves the way I designed the
   jet (no gravity, moves one level at a time).

3. I made my gates rock spikes (one from the top, one from the bottom).

4. I made my jet fly in 9 fixed levels (lanes).
   With 9 lanes, the movement is fair and easy to read.

5. I added keyboard controls.
   The Up/Down arrows (or W/S) work as well as clicking.

6. I space my gates by distance, not by time.
   This keeps the gates the same distance apart when the game speeds up, so
   the game gets harder smoothly instead of becoming impossible.

7. I stop the speed-up after 20 gates.
   I planned for the game to keep getting faster, but in testing it became
   unplayable. Now the speed stays the same after 20 gates, so a good player
   can score 100 or more.

8. I added a click ripple.
   A soft ring appears wherever I click, so people watching my video can see
   whether I clicked the top half or the bottom half.

9. I added small extras for game feel.
   My plane tilts when it changes lanes, leaves exhaust puffs, has a spinning
   propeller, and spins and falls when it crashes. A "NEW BEST!" label shows
   when the high score is beaten.

10. My sound list.
    Kept: engine, gate-pass, button, crash, and Game Over sounds.
    Added: a short sound when the plane changes lanes.
